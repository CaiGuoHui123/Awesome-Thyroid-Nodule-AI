# Awesome-Thyroid-Nodule-AI

A curated collection of artificial intelligence studies for thyroid nodule diagnosis, accompanying our review on technological evolution and research advances.

## About

This repository accompanies our review:

**Artificial Intelligence for Thyroid Nodule Diagnosis: Technological Evolution and Research Advances**

The repository collects representative studies on artificial intelligence for thyroid nodule diagnosis, covering the evolution from traditional machine learning and radiomics to deep learning, Transformers, multitask and multimodal learning, and emerging foundation-model approaches.

## Research Topics

The reviewed studies are organized into the following major categories:

- Detection & Localization
- Segmentation
- Classification & Malignancy Risk Prediction
- TI-RADS & Risk Stratification
- Multitask & Multimodal Learning
- Clinical Validation & Human–AI Collaboration
- Emerging Paradigms & Foundation Models

## Literature Coverage

The core literature reviewed in this work covers studies published from **January 2020 to July 2026**.

A total of **135 core studies** were included in the main review, together with additional foundational methodological papers used to describe the development of major artificial intelligence architectures.
