# Awesome-Thyroid-Nodule-AI

A curated collection of artificial intelligence studies for thyroid nodule diagnosis, accompanying our review on technological evolution and research advances.

## About

This repository accompanies our review:

**Artificial Intelligence for Thyroid Nodule Diagnosis: Technological Evolution and Research Advances**

Author: [Guohui Cai](https://github.com/CaiGuoHui123), et al.

Artificial intelligence for thyroid nodule analysis has evolved from handcrafted-feature-based machine learning and radiomics to convolutional neural networks, object detection and segmentation architectures, attention mechanisms, Transformers, multitask and multimodal learning, and more recently weakly supervised learning, federated learning, Mamba-based architectures, vision-language models, and large language models.

This repository provides a curated collection of representative studies reviewed in our work and organizes them according to their primary technical or clinical research focus.

Studies involving multiple tasks are assigned to their **primary research objective** for counting purposes, while their secondary contributions may also be discussed across related sections of the review.

Foundational methodological papers published outside the main literature-search period are additionally included where necessary to explain the origins and development of major artificial intelligence architectures.

**These foundational methodological papers are not included in the 135 core studies.**

> **2026 coverage:** Studies published or available online in 2026 are included only through **July 2026**. Therefore, 2026 does not represent a complete calendar year.

# Major Clinical Tasks

Artificial intelligence systems for thyroid nodule analysis have been developed for several interconnected clinical tasks:

### Detection & Localization

Automatically identifying suspicious nodules from complete ultrasound images or dynamic ultrasound examinations.

### Segmentation

Delineating thyroid nodule boundaries at the pixel level to support quantitative analysis and subsequent diagnosis.

### Classification & Risk Prediction

Distinguishing benign from malignant nodules and estimating malignancy-related clinical risks.

### TI-RADS & Risk Stratification

Automatically recognizing ultrasound characteristics and supporting standardized TI-RADS-oriented risk assessment.

### Multitask & Multimodal Analysis

Combining related diagnostic tasks or integrating complementary imaging and clinical information.

### Clinical Decision Support

Evaluating AI systems in external datasets, comparing AI with physicians, and investigating human-AI collaborative workflows.

# Current Challenges

Despite rapid progress, several challenges remain before thyroid nodule AI systems can be widely translated into clinical practice:

- Limited multicenter and prospective validation
- Dataset heterogeneity
- Differences in ultrasound devices and acquisition protocols
- Patient-level data leakage risks
- Limited representation of rare pathological subtypes
- Small or imbalanced datasets
- Inconsistent annotation standards
- Limited model interpretability
- Insufficient calibration analysis
- Cross-device and cross-center domain shift
- Limited reporting of computational efficiency
- Limited real-world workflow evidence
- Incomplete reproducibility and open-data availability
